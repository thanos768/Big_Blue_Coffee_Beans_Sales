# Big_Blue_Coffee_Beans_Sales

Members:
Thanos Chronopoulos, Andreas Gabriel, Filippos Georgiopoulos 

Project name:
Sales Analysis in Coffee beans

Description: 
This project proposes a comprehensive analysis of coffee bean sales data targeting three key international markets: the United States, the United Kingdom, and Ireland.

The primary objectives of this study are threefold:

Trend Identification: To identify and analyze prevailing sales trends concerning both coffee type (Liberica, Excelsa, Arabica and Robusta) and roast type (e.g., light, medium, dark) across the target countries. Additionally, we will investigate the impact of the loyalty card system on sales to determine if the program significantly drives revenue or customer purchasing behavior.

Comparative Revenue Analysis (2019–2022): To compare and assess the annual revenue generated from coffee bean sales over the four-year period from 2019 to 2022. This analysis will seek to understand the performance dynamics, quantify growth or decline, and investigate potential underlying causal factors influencing observed revenue fluctuations (such as Brexit and COVID-19).

Temporal Revenue Performance: To examine and compare revenue generation patterns based on day of the week and month of the year. This involves analyzing and contrasting the revenue generated on specific weekdays and months within the total dataset against the average performance of those same temporal units across all years in the study period.
